# M07 review follow-up

The external review of PR #19 arrived immediately before the planned merge on 2026-11-02. The merge was not executed. Findings were checked against current code, with these changes prepared for fresh verification:

- Dispute evidence grants now require a non-null matching conversation reference, preventing a grant for a booking without chat from authorizing another booking’s photo. Removed-message photos are denied to participants on both new ticket requests and downloads; justified staff audit remains scoped to the authorized record.
- Media idempotency records retain acknowledgements; every ticket retry obtains a fresh permission check and retention-bounded deadline. Expired audit-opening retries return an explicit expiry error, and the browser drops that obsolete request key.
- Upload retry controls use button semantics. Composers and work forms block submission while uploads or photo withdrawal are pending; upload controls also pause during a submission. Completion summary/body lengths match the server, photo-backed summaries use the valid shorter minimum, and old success text is cleared before another attempt.
- Open conversations poll bounded tombstone pages for loaded history as well as new messages. A late message response cannot restore tombstoned content or attachments.
- Inbox and support queues have cursor paging. The support queue deduplicates conversation references before paging. Audit photos are bounded by the current message page; safety reports and work history have independent earlier-page controls. The initial work view contains the newest 200 visible entries, preserving recent corrections and support notes.
- Audit selection changes invalidate pending responses and clear the previous view. Triage has its own explicit reason; old access-reason state is not reused as the triage justification. Conversation actions are hidden for declined/cancelled appointments. Asset duplication checks compare UUIDs without letter-case differences.

New regression coverage includes actor/session and cross-booking evidence denial, expired-ticket replay, moderated-message polling/file denial, upload retry without implicit submission, blocked submit during native upload, a photo-backed completion through the browser, delayed audit response after selection changes, inbox/queue/report/work-history pages beyond their first window, retained held bytes after cleanup, and zero financial intents for each lifecycle fixture. Synthetic restored historical rows exercise pagination limits; they do not claim real visits, approvals or safety reports. Final executed outcomes belong in the PR release record; this note does not claim checks before they run.
